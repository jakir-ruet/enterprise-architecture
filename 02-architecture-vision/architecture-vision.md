# Architecture Vision

## Purpose

Define the desired architectural direction for the Ads Promotional System and establish the scope for subsequent ADM phases.

## System

- Backend: `ads-promo-api`
- Frontend: `ads-promo-web`

## Business Scope

Current architecture scope includes:

- customer management
- advertisement management
- campaign management
- promotion management
- media management
- reporting
- audit
- user/role/permission management
- customer messaging / WhatsApp-related capabilities

## Vision

Provide a controlled, maintainable and scalable platform for managing advertisement and promotional business capabilities while enabling secure integration with external systems and reporting channels.

## Architecture Drivers

- business process efficiency
- maintainability
- integration
- security
- operational visibility
- scalability
- controlled change
- cost awareness

## Success Measures

Measures should be agreed with business and technology stakeholders. Candidate measures include:

- reduced manual processing
- improved processing time
- improved availability
- reduced deployment effort
- reduced incident resolution time
- improved auditability

> Quantitative targets are **To Validate**.
